# The 100-Day Transformation Program

> **This file is the source of truth.** Every evening session reads this document to build the next day's page. Do not improvise workouts that contradict this program — adapt intensity based on the user's feedback, but keep the structure.

## Who this is for

Yashas — 28M, 5'11" (180cm), ~70kg, classic skinny-fat. Software engineer, works 11:00–18:30 with 1–2h daily commute. Weekday training window: **30–40 minutes at home**. Weekends: gym access.

**Baseline (Day 0):** 3 push-ups (wrist pain limits), 0 pull-ups, 20 bodyweight squats, 35s plank. Right wrist hurts in extended (palm-flat) push-up position — old badminton injury.

**Equipment:** pair of fixed 5kg dumbbells, doorway pull-up bar, resistance band set (light/medium/heavy loop or tube bands).

**Goal:** body recomposition, not weight loss. Broad shoulders, visible chest, back/V-taper, defined arms, flat stomach, fixed posture. "Lean athletic guy." The scale may barely move — that's fine and expected.

**Priorities:** shoulders > chest > back/lats > arms > core > legs (maintain).

---

## The honest expectations talk

100 days of consistent training as a detrained beginner buys you the fastest muscle growth you will ever experience ("newbie gains") — but be realistic:

- **You will NOT have a dramatic before/after by Day 100.** You WILL have visibly rounder shoulders, a chest that exists, arms that fill sleeves slightly, notably better posture, and a flatter (not shredded) stomach.
- **Strength moves fast, size moves slow.** Expect push-ups 3→20+, pull-ups 0→3–5, and that strength curve is the leading indicator that size is coming in months 4–8.
- **Abs by Day 100 is unlikely at ~70kg skinny-fat.** A flat stomach and top-two-abs-in-good-lighting is realistic. Full abs = a later phase.
- **The real prize is Day 101:** you'll have the habits, the strength base, and the knowledge to keep going. This program is designed for adherence first, optimization second.

**Common mistakes to avoid (all phases):**
1. Skipping a day, then "making up" with a double session. Never. Missed = skipped, move on.
2. Chasing soreness. Soreness ≠ progress; the log book (reps/weight going up) = progress.
3. Under-eating protein and over-restricting calories. You're recomping, not cutting.
4. Testing maxes ("let me see if I can do a pull-up yet") outside scheduled test days. It steals recovery.
5. Ignoring the wrist/posture work because it's boring. It's the difference between finishing and quitting at Day 40.

## Phase map

| Phase | Days | Name | Job |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| 1 | 1–14 | Foundation & Rehab | Recondition safely, fix wrist, groove movements, lock in habits |
| 2 | 15–42 | Build | Progressive overload begins; volume climbs; first strength milestones |
| 3 | 43–70 | Progress | Peak volume; first full pull-up; visible changes start. **Deload days 50–56** |
| 4 | 71–100 | Peak & Consolidate | Best-shape push, habit audit, exit plan. **Deload days 85–91** |

**Why phases exist:** a detrained body adapts fastest to the thing it did most recently — so we re-teach movement first (Phase 1), then add load stress gradually (Phase 2), push hardest when tissues are ready (Phase 3), and consolidate before exiting (Phase 4). Deloads are scheduled BEFORE you feel you need them; that's what keeps joints and motivation intact.

## Warm-up (before every session, 5 min)

1. Arm circles — 10 each direction
2. Band pull-aparts — 15
3. Cat-cow — 8
4. Scapular wall slides — 8
5. Wrist circles + prayer stretch — 30s each
6. 10 bodyweight squats

## Posture routine (daily, 5 min) — "The Desk Undo"

1. **Chin tucks** — 10 reps, 2s hold (forward head)
2. **Doorway pec stretch** — 45s each side (rounded shoulders)
3. **Band pull-aparts** — 2×15 (rear delts/upper back)
4. **Thoracic extension over chair back or foam roller** — 60s
5. **Wall angels** — 8 slow reps

Plus workday micro-habit: every time you get water at office, do 5 chin tucks + shoulder rolls.

## Wrist rehab protocol (daily in Phases 1–2, 5 min)

The wrist hurts in extension under load (palm flat, knuckles back). Strategy: avoid painful ranges (neutral-wrist pushing on dumbbell handles), strengthen forearms, restore extension range gradually.

1. **Wrist circles** — 20s each direction
2. **Prayer stretch** — 3×20s (gentle, no pain)
3. **Reverse prayer / palm-back stretch** — 2×15s (only to mild stretch)
4. **Wrist curls with 5kg dumbbell** — 2×15 (palm up, forearm on thigh)
5. **Reverse wrist curls with 5kg (or 2.5 if too heavy — hold the head)** — 2×12
6. **Weight-bearing progression** (one step per week as tolerated, NO pain allowed):
   wall lean palms flat → table lean → hands-on-knees floor → full floor quadruped rocking
7. **Rule for all pushing exercises:** use **push-up handles / dumbbell handles (neutral grip)**. If any rep causes wrist pain, switch to the fist/knuckle or handle variant. Pain = stop that variant, never push through.

## Exercise progressions (the spine of the program)

Each movement has a ladder. **Advance to the next rung when you hit 3 sets × 12 clean reps** (unless stated). Log every session; next session, try to beat the log by 1–2 reps or advance the rung.

### Push-up ladder (wrist-safe: always on dumbbell handles or fists)
1. Wall push-up → 2. Incline push-up (table/desk height) → 3. Incline (low, sofa arm/bench) → 4. Knee push-up → 5. Full push-up → 6. Feet-elevated push-up → 7. (later) weighted/archer

### Pull-up ladder (doorway bar)
1. Dead hang (build to 3×30s) → 2. Scapular pulls 3×8 → 3. Negatives (jump up, 3–5s lower) 3×5 → 4. Band-assisted pull-ups 3×5 → 5. Full pull-ups (singles → sets) → 6. 3×5 full

### Pike push-up ladder (shoulders)
1. Pike push-up, knees bent, small range → 2. Full pike push-up → 3. Feet-elevated pike → 4. (later) wall handstand hold

### Row ladder
1. Band row (seated/standing) → 2. Single-arm 5kg dumbbell row (slow tempo, 3-1-3) → 3. Both DBs backpack-loaded row → 4. Inverted row under sturdy table (if available) / heavy band row

### Plank ladder
1. Plank (add 5s per week from 35s baseline) → 2. 60s+ → switch to harder: plank shoulder taps → 3. Long-lever plank → RKC plank

### Squat/hinge (maintenance)
1. Bodyweight squat 3×15 → 2. Tempo squat (3s down) → 3. 5kg goblet/DB squats → 4. Bulgarian split squat (bodyweight → 5kg)
Hinge: 5kg Romanian deadlift (both DBs) 3×12–15, focus on hamstring stretch feel.

### With 5kg dumbbells (accessories)
- Lateral raise: 3×12–20 (5kg will be heavy at first, perfect for months)
- Overhead press (neutral grip): 3×8–15 → then slow tempo when 15 is easy
- Curls: 3×10–15 → then slow tempo/pause
- Overhead triceps extension (single DB, both hands): 3×12–15
- Band: face pulls 3×15 (daily-ish — posture king), band OHP, band triceps pushdown, band lateral raise (combine with DB for drop sets later)

## The sessions (weekday home, by phase)

Format: exercises in order. Rest 60–90s between sets (30s for bands/isolation). All pushing on handles/fists (wrist rule).

### PHASE 1 (Days 1–14) — "Show up, don't blow up"
Intensity cap: stop every set 3+ reps before failure. The goal is showing up 14 days straight and grooving patterns.

**Push A (shoulders):** Pike push-up rung 1 3×6–10 · Band OHP 3×12 · 5kg lateral raise 3×10–12 · Band face pull 3×15 · Wall/incline push-up (current rung) 3×8–12
**Pull A (back):** Dead hang 3×max(≤30s) · Scapular pulls 3×5–8 · Band row 3×12–15 · Band pull-apart 3×15 · 5kg curls 2×12
**Push B (chest/arms):** Push-up ladder current rung 4×8–12 · 5kg floor press 3×12 · 5kg overhead triceps ext 3×12 · Band triceps pushdown 2×15 · 5kg curls 2×12
**Pull B + core:** Band row 3×15 · Single-arm 5kg row 3×12/side · Face pull 3×15 · Plank 3×(baseline+5s) · Dead bug 3×8/side
**Sat Gym (technique day):** Lat pulldown 3×12 light · Machine chest press 3×12 light · Seated cable row 3×12 · Machine shoulder press 3×12 · Leg press 3×12 — learn the machines, film nothing, ego nowhere.
**Sun:** full rest (Phase 1).

### PHASE 2 (Days 15–42) — "Build"
Intensity: 1–2 reps in reserve. Progression rule active: beat the log.

**Push A:** Pike push-up (advance rungs) 4×8–12 · 5kg OHP slow tempo 3×10–15 · 5kg lateral raise 4×12–15 · Band face pull 3×15 · Push-up rung 3×AMRAP(-1)
**Pull A:** Pull-up ladder work (hangs→scap pulls→negatives as earned) 4 sets · Band/DB row 4×10–15 · Band pull-apart 3×20 · 5kg curls 3×12
**Push B:** Push-up ladder 4×8–12 · 5kg floor press slow 3×12–15 · 5kg overhead triceps 3×12–15 · Band pushdown 3×15 · Curls 3×12 superset band curls
**Pull B + core:** Single-arm row slow tempo 4×12 · Face pull 3×20 · Rear-delt band fly 3×15 · Plank ladder 3 sets · Dead bug/bird dog 3×10
**Sat Gym Upper (heavy):** Barbell/DB bench 4×6–10 · Barbell/cable row 4×8–10 · OHP (bar/DB) 3×8–10 · Lat pulldown 3×10–12 · Cable lateral raise 2×15 · Face pull 3×15
**Sun Gym Lower + core:** Leg press or goblet squat 4×10–12 · Romanian deadlift 3×10 · Leg curl 3×12 · Calf raise 3×15 · Cable crunch or hanging knee raise 3×12

### PHASE 3 (Days 43–70) — "Progress" (deload days 50–56)
Intensity: 0–1 reps in reserve on last set. Volume peak. **First pull-up test: Day 60 (only then).**

Same session skeleton as Phase 2 with: 5th set on first exercise · tempo/pause variants where 5kg is too light (3-1-3 tempo, 1.5 reps on lateral raises) · pull-up ladder priority — negatives/band-assisted 5 sets on Pull A · push-up target: full push-ups 3×8–12 by end of phase · plank → shoulder taps/long-lever.
**Deload (50–56):** all sessions at half sets, 2 rungs easier, gym optional light. Posture/wrist/walking unchanged. This is planned recovery — growth happens here.

### PHASE 4 (Days 71–100) — "Peak & Consolidate" (deload days 85–91)
Intensity: push hard weeks 71–84, deload 85–91, then 92–100 "victory lap" — moderate sessions + testing + photos.
- Weeks 11–12: same skeleton, add intensity techniques on LAST set only: drop set (DB→band) on laterals, AMRAP on push-ups/rows.
- **Day 92–100 tests, one per session:** max push-ups · max pull-ups · max plank · 5kg OHP max reps · measurements + photos Day 100.
- Exit planning: last week's pages include "what's next" coaching (see Day 101 section).

## Nutrition

**Targets (every day, training or rest):**
- **Calories: ~2,350 kcal** (maintenance-ish; recomp happens via training + protein, not deficit)
- **Protein: 135g** (non-negotiable, the #1 nutrition metric)
- Fat: ~65g · Carbs: ~280g (soft targets, don't micro-track)
- Water: 3L (10 × 300ml glasses)

**Why no deficit:** at 70kg/5'11" you don't have a calorie problem, you have a muscle problem. Eat at maintenance, train hard, and the belly shrinks as the shoulders grow. If waist hasn't budged by Day 45 review, drop to 2,200.

### Meal skeleton — built on the real diet (updated Day 1)

His actual base (Bengaluru, South Karnataka): rice + sambar 2–3×/day, **ragi mudde every
night**, chicken 2–3×/week, 2 eggs added at breakfast. Don't fight this — protein gets
bolted ONTO it. Ragi is good food (calcium, iron, satiety, ~7g protein per 100g dry) but
it is NOT a protein source — one mudde ≈ 7g protein and ~340 kcal. Extra mudde = extra
calories, not extra muscle. One mudde a night, always paired with a protein main.

| Meal | Target | The build |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Breakfast | 20–25g | 3 eggs (up from 2) + usual base (idli/dosa/toast). Egg #4 when hungry. |
| Lunch | 30–40g | Rice + **thick, dal-heavy sambar** + 2 boiled eggs on the side + curd 200g |
| Snack | 15–25g | Whey when it arrives (1 scoop = 24g) · until then: milk 250ml + peanuts / sprouts usali |
| Dinner | 35–45g | Ragi mudde ×1 + the protein main: chicken curry 150g (target 4–5 nights/wk) · egg curry (3 eggs) · paneer 150g · **soya-chunk curry (40g dry = ~20g protein — the cheapest protein in India, takes up sambar masala beautifully)** |

**Grocery staples:** eggs (3 dozen/wk), chicken 1kg/wk, curd daily, toor dal (thick sambar),
soya chunks (₹60/250g — learn one curry), paneer, sprouts/moong, peanuts, milk, whey
(1 scoop/day non-negotiable once it arrives), ragi flour as usual.
**Eating out rule:** order protein first (tandoori/grill anything), skip the sugary drink, done. No guilt, no "restart Monday."
**Commute-proofing:** breakfast and snack are the two meals that die to a busy day — prep boiled eggs in batches of 6, keep whey + shaker at office.

## Recovery rules

- **Sleep 7h+.** With an 11:00 start you have no excuse; screens off by 00:30.
- Wed + (Phase 1/deloads) Sun are rest days. Rest means rest + the 10-min posture/wrist routine.
- Two consecutive terrible-sleep days or illness → auto-downgrade the day to a walk + posture routine. The page for the next day should ask and adapt.
- Wrist pain anywhere above 2/10 during pushing → drop to previous rung + extra rehab set. Persistent pain past Day 30 → see a physio (seriously).

## Day 101 (write this into the final week's pages)

The program's real success metric: on Day 101 he joins a gym properly or continues the home program at higher volume, keeps 135g protein as default life, and keeps the posture routine forever. The last week's coaching notes should draft "the next 100" together with him.
